Happy New Year!

Dec 31, 2024 • Matt

Happy New Year! 🎉 I wanted to share a short update about the latest Beta version of Buckets.

Bug fixes and features¶

Buckets v0.78.0 was released last week and includes several fixes for issues lingering from v0.76.0. It was these issues that have prevented releasing these changes in the stable version. Now that they’re fixed, expect a stable version soon!

Oh, and it also includes the first draft of dark mode!

And, you can now categorize multiple transactions at once. Combined with the new filtering/search/sorting features, this can make categorizing much faster. It has for us.

Thank you!¶

Thank you to everyone who has purchased a license in support of Buckets. It makes a big difference and we appreciate it.

Happy budgeting!

— Matt
